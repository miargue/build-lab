Problem

A missed handover can delay real shipments. Sticky notes, Slack threads, and verbal handoffs leave details scattered — and in a warehouse, the person who walks in blind is the one who can't find the urgent item.

Solution

HandOff keeps the current state of the shift front and center. The outgoing lead updates the board throughout the shift, whenever something matters. The incoming lead reads the truth at a glance — no scrolling, no digging, no guesswork.

  • Status at a glance — green means steady, amber means watch, red means handle it
  • Notes in seconds — capture the important detail the moment it happens
  • No history, no clutter — just the current state, exactly where you expect it
